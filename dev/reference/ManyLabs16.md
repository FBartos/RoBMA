# 55 effect sizes from Many Labs 2 replication studies of Tversky and Kahneman (1981) framing effects

The data set contains standardized mean differences (y) and standard
errors (se) from 55 replication studies of the framing effect originally
described by Tversky and Kahneman (1981) . These studies were part of
the Many Labs 2 project examining variation in replicability across
samples and settings Klein et al. (2018) .

## Usage

``` r
ManyLabs16
```

## Format

A data.frame with 2 columns and 55 observations:

- `y`:

  Standardized mean difference.

- `se`:

  Standard error of y.
