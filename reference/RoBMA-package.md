# RoBMA: Robust Bayesian meta-analysis

RoBMA: Bayesian model-averaged meta-analysis with adjustments for
publication bias and ability to specify informed prior distributions and
draw inference with inclusion Bayes factors.

## User guide

See Bartoš et al. (2023) , Maier et al. (2023) , and Bartoš et al.
(2022) for details regarding the RoBMA methodology.

More details regarding customization of the model ensembles are provided
in the [**Reproducing BMA**](doc/ReproducingBMA.md), [**BMA in
Medicine**](doc/MedicineBMA.md), and [**Fitting Custom Meta-Analytic
Ensembles**](doc/CustomEnsembles.md) vignettes. Please, use the "Issues"
section in the GitHub repository to ask any further questions.
